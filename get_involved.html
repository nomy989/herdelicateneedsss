Get Involved

There are several ways in which you can support the organisation and help make a difference in girls and women’s lives.

Donate

Your donation can help supply hygiene products and sanitary products to women and girls who can't afford to purchase them. Your donation will also help teach women and girls about menstrual health and support school programmes.

We accept every donation, no matter how small. Your support can help women and girls in South Africa manage their periods in dignity.

Your donation can help

Provide hygiene and sanitary products to women and girls who are in need in communities.

Let people know about period poverty.

Help teach people about menstrual health.

Make your Donation Today

Choose any amount you would like to donate and help the organisation in its mission to get rid of period poverty.

Volunteer

Volunteers help the organisation with school programmes, menstrual health campaigns, fundraising events and community projects. By giving your time, you can help Her Delicate Needs reach more women and girls who need support.

Volunteer Opportunities include

School programmes

Menstrual health campaigns

Community work

Fundraising events

Awareness campaigns

If you would like to volunteer, contact us and tell us how you would like to help.

Become Partner

Schools, businesses, healthcare organisations and universities can help Her Delicate Needs in its mission to fight period poverty. Partners can provide resources, educational support and financial help or help Her Delicate Needs reach more people who need support.

By becoming a partner, you can

Give support to women and girls who are experiencing period poverty.

Support women and girls by teaching them about menstrual health.

Help provide sanitary and hygiene products to women and girls.

Make a positive impact on the community.

Fundraising

You can help the organisation by organising a fundraising event in your community, school or university.The money raised can help provide sanitary and hygiene products, support educational programmes and reach more communities in South Africa affected by period poverty.

Fundraising ideas include

School and community fundraising events

Collecting sanitary products

Online fundraising

Get Involved Today

Everyone can make a difference. You can donate, fundraise, volunteer, or become a partner to help the organisation help more women and girls in South Africa.
