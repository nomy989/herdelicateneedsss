About

Her Delicate Needs is a volunteer organisation created by women, for women and girls, with a strong commitment to supporting girls and women living in underprivileged communities.

What We Do

We support communities by

-Providing them with sanitary products

-Supporting girls through educational programmes

-Community Outreach

-Educating women ang girls about menstrual hygiene

How Can You Help

You can help make an impact by volunteering fundraisingS,becoming a partner and donating.
