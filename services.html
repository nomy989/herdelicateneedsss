Services

The organisation offers various services that help women and girls manage their menstrual health.

Menstrual Education

We teach women and girls about menstrual hygiene, periods, and how to manage their health safely during their menstrual cycles

Sanitary and hygiene product support

We provide hygiene and sanitary product support to women and girls who do not have afford or have access to them.

Community Projects

We work with community clinics and hospitals to raise awareness about period poverty and provide support to those who need it.

School Visits

We work with schools in communities to support young girls and educate them about menstrual health and hygiene.
