Introduction

Her Delicate Needs is a volunteer organisation created by
women, for women and girls, with a strong commitment to supporting
girls and women living in underprivileged communities. This organisation knows that having access to basic sanitary products can uplift a woman's confidence and overall well-being. Unfortunately,
a lot of women and girls from disadvantaged communities cannot afford these products, resulting in health challenges and missed school days (Jacobs, 2025).  Her Delicate Needs wants to address this issue by serving underprivileged communities by supplying sanitary pads, tampons, and other hygiene essentials while offering educational sessions on menstruation, puberty, personal hygiene, and women’s health.
Her Delicate Needs believes that menstrual health should never be a reason a girl cannot go to school or feel ashamed. Through collaboration, donations and fundraising events, Her Delicate Needs believes it has helped a lot of communities by distributing sanitary products through schools, educating young girls about their bodies and also making sure that every female bathroom in the community has sanitary products.
